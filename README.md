# 🍓 Snack Buddy

A chatbot that matches your mood and cravings to the perfect snack. Vibe coded and designed for mobile first.

🍪 **[Find your snack](https://soumyag777.github.io/Snack-bot/)**

## Features
- 🧠 **Smart matching:** 18 snacks, each tagged by mood and flavor, and the bot scores them against what you type
- 💬 **Natural input:** try "stressed and want something crunchy" or "tired, need something sweet"
- 🔁 **"Another one"** remembers your vibe and suggests something new without repeats
- 🏷️ **Snack cards** show exactly why each pick matches you
- 🍓 The logo turns into your snack and does a little munch

## Built with
HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, split into `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Design notes
Showing *why* a snack was picked ("perfect for: stressed, crunchy") makes the suggestion feel personal instead of random, and builds trust in the recommendation.
